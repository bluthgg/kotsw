The Venomous Abyss · Boss 8 · Heroic

Ula'tek

Raid plan for the guild. Two groups, Odds and Evens, run the whole fight. Pick your group and the page dims what you can ignore. Names from the roster below show up in the diagrams.

Raid roster

Set the raid size, type names, and tap a group button to move someone between Odds and Evens.

Tap the egg next to a healer, melee or ranged player to make them the Phase 2 big egg carrier for their group. One per group. Tap again to clear it.

Before the pull

  • Split the raid into two even groups, Odds and Evens. You stay in the same group for the whole fight.
  • Each group has one tank. The Odds tank leads Odds and the Evens tank leads Evens. This matters in the intermission.
  • Use Heroism when the second set of adds spawns in Phase 1.
  • Save your potion and cooldowns for the first burn phase, the Heart at around 2 minutes in. Plan them in advance.
  • Pick up eggs by walking over them. Rawlings are harmless, but Vipers can wipe the raid. A Viper spawns if you are holding an egg and get hit by a wave. If one spawns, DPS must kill it right away.
  • In Phase 2, assign one player with a strong movement ability per group to carry the big egg. Pick them in the roster below with the egg button.
  • Heart phase breakpoints: first Heart phase below 50% boss health, second Heart phase below 30%. Close to those numbers works too, but these usually guarantee the kill and keep you from reaching the third Heart phase in Phase 3.
  • Tanks: whenever Ula'tek is up, one tank must stay in melee range of her, or the raid wipes. Mother's Wrath knocks back, so the tank must already be standing in the red soak circle before it is cast.
  • Tanks: the tank who takes the next Mother's Wrath must taunt first, above all in phases where Ula'tek goes under the venom. Make sure you have aggro, or you are "not worthy" and cannot enter the red circle.

Important: Phase 3

In Phase 3, the soaks must be done properly. Do not panic. Make sure ranged and healers split the soaks well so they get done in a timely fashion. Missing a soak wipes the raid. If you are heavy on melee, assign Retribution Paladins or Death Knights to one of the soaks. They have enough range to keep doing damage during that phase.

Phase 1

Group up away from her head turn. Odds soak the tail's right coil first, Evens the left second. Burst the Heart at 2 minutes.

Phase 2

Odds go left, Evens go right. Warlock gate across the venom. Pull the Warden to the line, snap tethers in turns, then feed the cauldron.

Intermission

Carry eggs to your soaks and kill the adds. Odds soak casts 1, 3, 5, Evens 2, 4, 6. Hold the middle for the shattering.

Phase 3

Kill eggs and adds. Melee soak on her platform, healers and ranged on the platform to her left, with extra ranged on the healer soak.

Phase 1

Fury of the Serpent Mother

Keep every egg safe, group up for the waves, soak the tail in order, and arrive at the Heart phase with the adds ready to cleave.

Our calls

  • OddsTake the first Spectral Coils soak, the tail's right coil. Everyone stays mixed until the coils.
  • EvensTake the second soak, the tail's left coil. The tail can pop up on either side. The order and timing stay the same.
  • AllPick up every egg. If a wave hits you while you hold an egg, a Viper spawns. If one does, nuke it down.
  • AllWaves: Ula'tek turns her head toward a side and the waves go that way. Shift to the opposite side of her, but stay close to Ula'tek, and group up. There are two of these per wave phase, and she always turns the opposite way for the second one. Which way comes first is random. The tail's waves are smaller.
  • TanksSwap after the first waves. Swap again after the next Mother's Wrath. Mother's Wrath leaves a slow. Remove it with a movement ability, like Druid shapeshift or Paladin Freedom.
  • AllAfter the first soak the Tail uses Mephitic Thrash. It hits a half circle in front of the Tail, so move out of it. Then Ula'tek and the Tail submerge and re-emerge, Ula'tek on the west and the Tail on the north. From then on, face the Tail from the center: Odds soak right (east), Evens left (west).
  • AllAfter the first soak, eggs spawn a second time. Pick them up, do another wave phase, then the second Spectral Coils spawns all the adds.
  • AllThe moment those adds spawn, use Heroism. The tank moves them quickly to Ula'tek so they are ready to cleave for the Heart.
  • AllThe Heart phase usually starts right at 2 minutes. Save your potion for it. The Heart takes 100% more damage for 20 seconds.

Mechanics

Spectral Coils
Two soak circles on either side of the Tail. The Tail smashes them one after another, right first. Held eggs pop on the soak, which is intended.
Caustic Waves
She turns her head to telegraph the side. Stay close to her, on the other side, grouped. Two big waves per phase plus small ones from the Tail.
Call of the Serpent
More eggs fall. They hurt when they land. Pick them up.
Mephitic Thrash
Huge AoE around the Tail. The tank runs out and back in. Leave no eggs behind. Raid damage and a DoT follow.
Mother's Wrath
Tank buster with a knockback, slow and DoT. Use defensives. Remove the slow with movement abilities.
Unchecked Rage / Rattler Slam
Nobody in melee of Ula'tek, or nobody in melee of the Tail, means raid-wide damage.
Necrotic Vapors
Raid-wide rot damage all fight. Heal through it.
Rage of the Shackled
The Heart becomes attackable and takes 100% more damage. Falling debris lands in the dashed circles, so dodge it.

Phase 2

Children of the Doomscale

Split by group, pull the Warden to its line, break tethers in turns, then feed the cauldron and kill the adds.

TipHave Warlocks place a gateway across the venom gap. It makes crossing easy. Otherwise use movement abilities or swim across. Keep the gate for the trip back, since it lines up with the 1:30 cooldown.
TipUse a personal defensive while the tethers break. This matters most if the Warden dies while both sides are still breaking tethers. That DoT plus the rot damage from holding an egg can be deadly.

Our calls

  • OddsGo left. Cross the venom gap and climb the stairs at the back end of the ledge.
  • EvensGo right. Cross the venom gap and climb the stairs at the back end of the ledge.
  • TanksAt the very beginning, bring the Warden behind the small line by the stairs. That makes it bug, teleport or molt and snap onto a set of eggs that die quickly and sit close to the cauldron.
  • TanksInterrupt every Malice and keep the Warden near the attackable eggs and adds.
  • OddsYour 3 tethered players snap their tethers first, slowly and one at a time.
  • EvensWait for the Odds DoT to drop off, then snap your 3 tethers. The raid is not healing both DoTs at once.
  • AllDodge Virulent Spit swirlies. They hit very hard.
  • AllAfter the Warden dies, DPS, healers and tanks carry the small eggs (6 to 8) to the cauldron.
  • TanksStay at the cauldron and pick up the small adds.
  • AllAssign one player with a movement ability to grab the big egg at the very end of the ledge. Drop it in the cauldron only after every small egg is in.
  • AllDo not get hit by any green falling circle. It spawns an egg and makes the adds harder to kill.
  • AllWith strong DPS, kill every add, then take the Warlock gate back at 1:30. If DPS struggles, bring everything back to the main platform and kill it there.
  • AllBack on the main platform, burn the Heart during Rage of the Shackled. This is the last thing in Phase 2. Then Ula'tek dives under the venom and the intermission starts.
  • TanksThe big add has a frontal, so move out of the way and turn it away from the group. Interrupt its stun cast, or the whole raid is stunned.

Mechanics

Malice
Warden cast. Always interrupt.
Grasping Fangs
Three random players per side are tethered. It can be melee, ranged, healers or tanks. Snapping a tether causes a heavy DoT, so stagger them. The diagram has a Re-roll button to show different picks.
Virulent Spit
Swirlies with massive damage. Dodge.
Doomscale Cauldron
Eggs dropped in hatch weakened adds. The tank holds them at the cauldron. Picking up the big egg starts a timer that hatches everything, so move fast.
Green falling circles
Getting hit by one spawns an egg and makes the adds harder to kill. Stay out of them.
Anguished Cry / Desperate Trash
Interrupt the cry, or it stuns the whole raid. The big add's frontal points at the tank, so keep allies out of it.
Rage of the Shackled
Ends Phase 2. The Heart takes 100% more damage for 20 seconds. Burst it down. Then Ula'tek dives and the intermission starts.
Utility
Use grips and rescues on the way out. Save the Warlock gate for the trip back.

Intermission

The Shattering

Carry the eggs to your soaks, take every other smash with your tank, kill the adds, then hold the middle for the shattering.

Our calls

  • AllThe slams are not always next to each other. The order of the spots is a bit random, so they can jump across the platform. The diagram shows one example. Just make sure the groups take every other one: Odds 1, 3, 5 and Evens 2, 4, 6.
  • OddsFollow the Odds tank. Soak casts 1, 3 and 5.
  • EvensFollow the Evens tank. Soak casts 2, 4 and 6.
  • AllEggs spawn across the middle of the platform. Pick them up and carry them to your group's soak. The soak hatches adds. This happens a few times, so keep picking up eggs, soak with your tank and group, and kill the adds.
  • AllSoak, then kill your adds and run straight to your next spot with the next eggs.
  • AllWhen the platform shatters, stay close to the middle. The knock-up pushes you outward, and anyone near the edge lands in the venom. Take the fall into Phase 3.

Mechanics

Spectral Coils
The Tail appears above each spot before it smashes. A group has to be at every smash.
Spot order
The smashes can land anywhere on the platform and not always beside the last one. Follow your tank, and each group takes every other cast.
Eggs
Lots of eggs spawn across the middle during the run. Carry them into the soaks to hatch adds, then kill the adds.
Shattering
The platform breaks into 4 pieces and knocks everyone up. The knock-up pushes outward, so stay close to the middle to avoid landing in the venom.

Phase 3

Ula'tek's Ascension

Kill the eggs, kill the adds, interrupt Vicious Echoes, soak every Serpent's Bite, dodge the waves, and stay clear of Volatile Purge overlaps.

Important: Phase 3

In Phase 3, the soaks must be done properly. Do not panic. Make sure ranged and healers split the soaks well so they get done in a timely fashion. Missing a soak wipes the raid. If you are heavy on melee, assign Retribution Paladins or Death Knights to one of the soaks. They have enough range to keep doing damage during that phase.

Our calls

  • AllThe platform has broken into 4 pieces with venom in the middle and around them. Ula'tek rises in front of one piece, and the venom bubbles in front of that platform first. A Tail sits in the middle. You cannot target it, but it casts waves.The bubbles in the venom show where Ula'tek will pop up. The raid needs to pay attention to them.
  • MeleeStay on Ula'tek's platform. In the first egg phase, run to the platform to her right and kill that egg to hatch the add. Then move back to her platform and kill its egg. After the first platform breaks, melee only kills the egg on Ula'tek's platform.
  • RangedKill the other eggs. There may be 3 or 4. With 4 platforms up, melee takes the eggs to the right of her platform and on it. Once a platform is gone, ranged gets everything except her platform's egg.
  • Off-tankGrab the adds as they spawn, so everyone can nuke them down.
  • AllKill the adds and always interrupt Vicious Echoes.
  • MeleeSerpent's Bite: soak on Ula'tek's platform.
  • HealersSerpent's Bite: soak on the platform to the left of Ula'tek, on the left side.
  • RangedSerpent's Bite: soak on the platform to the left of Ula'tek, on the right side.
  • ExtraPut 3 or 4 players on the healer soak. Assign extra ranged to it if needed. The extra ranged named in the roster join the healer circle. If a soak fails, it is a wipe.
  • AllIf you have a Serpent's Bite soak, you do not get the individual Volatile Purge circle. Only the other players do.
  • AllAfter the bite, everyone gets Volatile Purge, except the three players who soaked the bite. The first time there is time before Circling Prey. Melee spreads their purge on Ula'tek's platform. Then Circling Prey breaks that platform and melee and tanks jump to the platform on the left. She always moves to the left after that.
  • AllOrbs spawn in front of Ula'tek's platform, after the bite and the Volatile Purge. Stand in the gaps between the orb lines. The orbs and the Tail each send single waves. They hurt a lot, so dodge them.
  • AllThe second time, Volatile Purge overlaps Circling Prey. Healers and ranged move to the back of their platform (the one to her left), so melee and tanks can jump over and spread their circles.
  • TanksSwap on the tank buster. It still happens in Phase 3. The tank taking the next Mother's Wrath taunts first and stands in the red soak circle before the cast. Keep a tank in melee range of Ula'tek at all times.
  • AllThe second Circling Prey is often a nasty Serpent's Bite straight into Volatile Purge with very little time to move. Use a defensive.
  • AllThere is a third Heart phase at the very end, on the last platform. It is the last hurrah. Burst before Circling Prey destroys the platform. Anyone left in the venom takes huge damage.

Mechanics

Serpent's Bite
Marks 1 melee, 1 healer and 1 ranged with a circle. Everyone soaks. Melee on her platform, healers and ranged on the platform to her left. A missed soak is a wipe.
Orbs and Tail waves
Orbs spawn on the front of her platform and each sends a single wave. The Tail also sends single waves. They deal a lot of damage, so dodge them.
Volatile Purge
Every player except the three who soaked the bite has a soak circle. Spread out. Avoid overlapping it with Circling Prey.
Circling Prey
Destroys the platform in front of Ula'tek. Move off before it breaks. The second one is the hardest part of the phase.
Blightscale Clutch / Acidic Explosion
Kill attackable eggs so the adds hatch and can be killed. Kill the adds fast. Tanks taunt them.
Vicious Echoes
Stuns the raid and hits hard. Never let it finish.
Third Heart phase
On the last platform the Heart takes extra damage. Burst before the platform breaks. Fury Unleashed is a huge raid-wide enrage.
