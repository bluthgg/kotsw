Phase 2

Children of the Doomscale

Split by group, pull the Warden to its line, break tethers in turns, then feed the cauldron and kill the adds.

TipHave Warlocks place a gateway across the venom gap. It makes crossing easy. Otherwise use movement abilities or swim across. Keep the gate for the trip back, since it lines up with the 1:30 cooldown.
TipUse a personal defensive while the tethers break. This matters most if the Warden dies while both sides are still breaking tethers. That DoT plus the rot damage from holding an egg can be deadly.

Our calls

  • OddsGo left. Cross the venom gap and climb the stairs at the back end of the ledge.
  • EvensGo right. Cross the venom gap and climb the stairs at the back end of the ledge.
  • TanksAt the very beginning, bring the Warden behind the small line by the stairs. That makes it bug, teleport or molt and snap onto a set of eggs that die quickly and sit close to the cauldron.
  • TanksInterrupt every Malice and keep the Warden near the attackable eggs and adds.
  • OddsYour 3 tethered players snap their tethers first, slowly and one at a time.
  • EvensWait for the Odds DoT to drop off, then snap your 3 tethers. The raid is not healing both DoTs at once.
  • AllDodge Virulent Spit swirlies. They hit very hard.
  • AllAfter the Warden dies, DPS, healers and tanks carry the small eggs (6 to 8) to the cauldron.
  • TanksStay at the cauldron and pick up the small adds.
  • AllAssign one player with a movement ability to grab the big egg at the very end of the ledge. Drop it in the cauldron only after every small egg is in.
  • AllDo not get hit by any green falling circle. It spawns an egg and makes the adds harder to kill.
  • AllWith strong DPS, kill every add, then take the Warlock gate back at 1:30. If DPS struggles, bring everything back to the main platform and kill it there.
  • AllBack on the main platform, burn the Heart during Rage of the Shackled. This is the last thing in Phase 2. Then Ula'tek dives under the venom and the intermission starts.
  • TanksThe big add has a frontal, so move out of the way and turn it away from the group. Interrupt its stun cast, or the whole raid is stunned.

Mechanics

Malice
Warden cast. Always interrupt.
Grasping Fangs
Three random players per side are tethered. It can be melee, ranged, healers or tanks. Snapping a tether causes a heavy DoT, so stagger them. The diagram has a Re-roll button to show different picks.
Virulent Spit
Swirlies with massive damage. Dodge.
Doomscale Cauldron
Eggs dropped in hatch weakened adds. The tank holds them at the cauldron. Picking up the big egg starts a timer that hatches everything, so move fast.
Green falling circles
Getting hit by one spawns an egg and makes the adds harder to kill. Stay out of them.
Anguished Cry / Desperate Trash
Interrupt the cry, or it stuns the whole raid. The big add's frontal points at the tank, so keep allies out of it.
Rage of the Shackled
Ends Phase 2. The Heart takes 100% more damage for 20 seconds. Burst it down. Then Ula'tek dives and the intermission starts.
Utility
Use grips and rescues on the way out. Save the Warlock gate for the trip back.
