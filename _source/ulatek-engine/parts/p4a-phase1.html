Phase 1

Fury of the Serpent Mother

Keep every egg safe, group up for the waves, soak the tail in order, and arrive at the Heart phase with the adds ready to cleave.

Our calls

  • OddsTake the first Spectral Coils soak, the tail's right coil. Everyone stays mixed until the coils.
  • EvensTake the second soak, the tail's left coil. The tail can pop up on either side. The order and timing stay the same.
  • AllPick up every egg. If a wave hits you while you hold an egg, a Viper spawns. If one does, nuke it down.
  • AllWaves: Ula'tek turns her head toward a side and the waves go that way. Shift to the opposite side of her, but stay close to Ula'tek, and group up. There are two of these per wave phase, and she always turns the opposite way for the second one. Which way comes first is random. The tail's waves are smaller.
  • TanksSwap after the first waves. Swap again after the next Mother's Wrath. Mother's Wrath leaves a slow. Remove it with a movement ability, like Druid shapeshift or Paladin Freedom.
  • AllAfter the first soak the Tail uses Mephitic Thrash. It hits a half circle in front of the Tail, so move out of it. Then Ula'tek and the Tail submerge and re-emerge, Ula'tek on the west and the Tail on the north. From then on, face the Tail from the center: Odds soak right (east), Evens left (west).
  • AllAfter the first soak, eggs spawn a second time. Pick them up, do another wave phase, then the second Spectral Coils spawns all the adds.
  • AllThe moment those adds spawn, use Heroism. The tank moves them quickly to Ula'tek so they are ready to cleave for the Heart.
  • AllThe Heart phase usually starts right at 2 minutes. Save your potion for it. The Heart takes 100% more damage for 20 seconds.

Mechanics

Spectral Coils
Two soak circles on either side of the Tail. The Tail smashes them one after another, right first. Held eggs pop on the soak, which is intended.
Caustic Waves
She turns her head to telegraph the side. Stay close to her, on the other side, grouped. Two big waves per phase plus small ones from the Tail.
Call of the Serpent
More eggs fall. They hurt when they land. Pick them up.
Mephitic Thrash
Huge AoE around the Tail. The tank runs out and back in. Leave no eggs behind. Raid damage and a DoT follow.
Mother's Wrath
Tank buster with a knockback, slow and DoT. Use defensives. Remove the slow with movement abilities.
Unchecked Rage / Rattler Slam
Nobody in melee of Ula'tek, or nobody in melee of the Tail, means raid-wide damage.
Necrotic Vapors
Raid-wide rot damage all fight. Heal through it.
Rage of the Shackled
The Heart becomes attackable and takes 100% more damage. Falling debris lands in the dashed circles, so dodge it.
