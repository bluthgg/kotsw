Intermission

The Shattering

Carry the eggs to your soaks, take every other smash with your tank, kill the adds, then hold the middle for the shattering.

Our calls

  • AllThe slams are not always next to each other. The order of the spots is a bit random, so they can jump across the platform. The diagram shows one example. Just make sure the groups take every other one: Odds 1, 3, 5 and Evens 2, 4, 6.
  • OddsFollow the Odds tank. Soak casts 1, 3 and 5.
  • EvensFollow the Evens tank. Soak casts 2, 4 and 6.
  • AllEggs spawn across the middle of the platform. Pick them up and carry them to your group's soak. The soak hatches adds. This happens a few times, so keep picking up eggs, soak with your tank and group, and kill the adds.
  • AllSoak, then kill your adds and run straight to your next spot with the next eggs.
  • AllWhen the platform shatters, stay close to the middle. The knock-up pushes you outward, and anyone near the edge lands in the venom. Take the fall into Phase 3.

Mechanics

Spectral Coils
The Tail appears above each spot before it smashes. A group has to be at every smash.
Spot order
The smashes can land anywhere on the platform and not always beside the last one. Follow your tank, and each group takes every other cast.
Eggs
Lots of eggs spawn across the middle during the run. Carry them into the soaks to hatch adds, then kill the adds.
Shattering
The platform breaks into 4 pieces and knocks everyone up. The knock-up pushes outward, so stay close to the middle to avoid landing in the venom.

Phase 3

Ula'tek's Ascension

Kill the eggs, kill the adds, interrupt Vicious Echoes, soak every Serpent's Bite, dodge the waves, and stay clear of Volatile Purge overlaps.

Important: Phase 3

In Phase 3, the soaks must be done properly. Do not panic. Make sure ranged and healers split the soaks well so they get done in a timely fashion. Missing a soak wipes the raid. If you are heavy on melee, assign Retribution Paladins or Death Knights to one of the soaks. They have enough range to keep doing damage during that phase.

Our calls

  • AllThe platform has broken into 4 pieces with venom in the middle and around them. Ula'tek rises in front of one piece, and the venom bubbles in front of that platform first. A Tail sits in the middle. You cannot target it, but it casts waves.The bubbles in the venom show where Ula'tek will pop up. The raid needs to pay attention to them.
  • MeleeStay on Ula'tek's platform. In the first egg phase, run to the platform to her right and kill that egg to hatch the add. Then move back to her platform and kill its egg. After the first platform breaks, melee only kills the egg on Ula'tek's platform.
  • RangedKill the other eggs. There may be 3 or 4. With 4 platforms up, melee takes the eggs to the right of her platform and on it. Once a platform is gone, ranged gets everything except her platform's egg.
  • Off-tankGrab the adds as they spawn, so everyone can nuke them down.
  • AllKill the adds and always interrupt Vicious Echoes.
  • MeleeSerpent's Bite: soak on Ula'tek's platform.
  • HealersSerpent's Bite: soak on the platform to the left of Ula'tek, on the left side.
  • RangedSerpent's Bite: soak on the platform to the left of Ula'tek, on the right side.
  • ExtraPut 3 or 4 players on the healer soak. Assign extra ranged to it if needed. The extra ranged named in the roster join the healer circle. If a soak fails, it is a wipe.
  • AllIf you have a Serpent's Bite soak, you do not get the individual Volatile Purge circle. Only the other players do.
  • AllAfter the bite, everyone gets Volatile Purge, except the three players who soaked the bite. The first time there is time before Circling Prey. Melee spreads their purge on Ula'tek's platform. Then Circling Prey breaks that platform and melee and tanks jump to the platform on the left. She always moves to the left after that.
  • AllOrbs spawn in front of Ula'tek's platform, after the bite and the Volatile Purge. Stand in the gaps between the orb lines. The orbs and the Tail each send single waves. They hurt a lot, so dodge them.
  • AllThe second time, Volatile Purge overlaps Circling Prey. Healers and ranged move to the back of their platform (the one to her left), so melee and tanks can jump over and spread their circles.
  • TanksSwap on the tank buster. It still happens in Phase 3. The tank taking the next Mother's Wrath taunts first and stands in the red soak circle before the cast. Keep a tank in melee range of Ula'tek at all times.

  • AllThere is a third Heart phase at the very end, on the last platform. It is the last hurrah. Burst before Circling Prey destroys the platform. Anyone left in the venom takes huge damage.

Mechanics

Serpent's Bite
Marks 1 melee, 1 healer and 1 ranged with a circle. Everyone soaks. Melee on her platform, healers and ranged on the platform to her left. A missed soak is a wipe.
Orbs and Tail waves
Orbs spawn on the front of her platform and each sends a single wave. The Tail also sends single waves. They deal a lot of damage, so dodge them.
Volatile Purge
Every player except the three who soaked the bite has a soak circle. Spread out. Avoid overlapping it with Circling Prey.
Circling Prey
Destroys the platform in front of Ula'tek. Move off before it breaks. The second one is the hardest part of the phase.
Blightscale Clutch / Acidic Explosion
Kill attackable eggs so the adds hatch and can be killed. Kill the adds fast. Tanks taunt them.
Vicious Echoes
Stuns the raid and hits hard. Never let it finish.
Third Heart phase
On the last platform the Heart takes extra damage. Burst before the platform breaks. Fury Unleashed is a huge raid-wide enrage.
