Before the pull

  • Split the raid into two even groups, Odds and Evens. You stay in the same group for the whole fight.
  • Each group has one tank. The Odds tank leads Odds and the Evens tank leads Evens. This matters in the intermission.
  • Use Heroism when the second set of adds spawns in Phase 1.
  • Save your potion and cooldowns for the first burn phase, the Heart at around 2 minutes in. Plan them in advance.
  • Pick up eggs by walking over them. Rawlings are harmless, but Vipers can wipe the raid. A Viper spawns if you are holding an egg and get hit by a wave. If one spawns, DPS must kill it right away.
  • In Phase 2, assign one player with a strong movement ability per group to carry the big egg. Pick them in the roster below with the egg button.
  • Heart phase breakpoints: first Heart phase below 50% boss health, second Heart phase below 30%. Close to those numbers works too, but these usually guarantee the kill and keep you from reaching the third Heart phase in Phase 3.
  • Tanks: whenever Ula'tek is up, one tank must stay in melee range of her, or the raid wipes. Mother's Wrath knocks back, so the tank must already be standing in the red soak circle before it is cast.
  • Tanks: the tank who takes the next Mother's Wrath must taunt first, above all in phases where Ula'tek goes under the venom. Make sure you have aggro, or you are "not worthy" and cannot enter the red circle.

Important: Phase 3

In Phase 3, the soaks must be done properly. Do not panic. Make sure ranged and healers split the soaks well so they get done in a timely fashion. Missing a soak wipes the raid. If you are heavy on melee, assign Retribution Paladins or Death Knights to one of the soaks. They have enough range to keep doing damage during that phase.

Phase 1

Group up away from her head turn. Odds soak the tail's right coil first, Evens the left second. Burst the Heart at 2 minutes.

Phase 2

Odds go left, Evens go right. Warlock gate across the venom. Pull the Warden to the line, snap tethers in turns, then feed the cauldron.

Intermission

Carry eggs to your soaks and kill the adds. Odds soak casts 1, 3, 5, Evens 2, 4, 6. Hold the middle for the shattering.

Phase 3

Kill eggs and adds. Melee soak on her platform, healers and ranged on the platform to her left, with extra ranged on the healer soak.
